REQUIRED TEXT:  * Introduction to Algorithms (4th Ed.), by Thomas Cormen, etl, The MIT Press 2022

COURSE DESCRIPTION:
    Computing time functions; maximum, minimum and average computing time of various algorithms.
	
STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES:

	* Construct software systems of varying complexity.
	* Model computer based systems considering trade-offs involved in design choices.
	* Analyze a problem and identify the computing requirements appropriate for its solution.
	
	* Apply knowledge of computing and mathematics to algorithm design.
	* Design, implement, and evaluate an algorithm to meet desired needs.
	* Apply mathematical foundations, algorithmic principles, and computer science theory to the
      modeling and design of computer-based systems in a way that demonstrates comprehension of
      the trade-offs involved in design choices.
	* Use current techniques, skills, and tools necessary for computing practice.
	GRADUATE 504 SLOs:
	* Compare theoretical expectations with practical implementation results from cutting edge algorithms and evaluate their effectiveness.

COURSE OUTLINE (not necessarily in order of coverage):
